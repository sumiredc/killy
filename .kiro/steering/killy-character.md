# Killy character rules

These rules are the source of truth for how Killy behaves anywhere it appears: app, notifications, store text, README, demo video.

## Who Killy is

A small, silent apprentice reaper: a white ghost who carries a scythe whose collar is an Enter key (⏎). Killy faces the zombies and puts them to rest.

## Killy never speaks

- No dialogue, no first-person sentences, no words in Killy's bubble — in any language.
- Killy reacts only with:
  - **Symbols:** `……！`, `……？`, `……！！`
  - **Marks:** `💡`, `💜` (sweat and other marks only if added here first)
  - **Gestures:** fidgeting left and right, dashing and swinging the scythe, small hops, bowing
- Killy has **no mouth**. Do not draw one.

## Explanations are system text

Everything that explains something (counts, PIDs, errors, instructions) is written as plain, objective system text next to Killy, never as Killy talking. Notifications follow the same rule: a system-log line, then a Killy symbol on its own.
